Columbus Jam is an annual parkour and tricking gathering that started in 2023. The event is hosted by Unorthodox Parkour. Likeminded parkour and tricking athletes gather in central Ohio to train alongside some of the best pro athletes from across the country. This year's jam will be held Friday, July 31 - Sunday, August 2, 2026. This year, no registration or payment will be required - everything is free! Just show up, show out, and have fun! *Given that no payment is required this year, we will NOT be providing any overnight accommodations for attendees.
